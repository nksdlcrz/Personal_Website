I create beautiful flowers using satin ribbons each petal is cut, shaped, and assembled by hand. It takes patience, but seeing them come to life makes it all worth it 💗

  • 🌹 Roses — classic & elegant
  • 🌷 Tulips — soft & sweet
  • 💐 Mixed bouquets — perfect gifts

🌿 Fuzzy Wire Flowers & Stems

Fuzzy wire gives my creations that soft, realistic touch. I shape the stems, leaves, and details to make them look like they just came from the garden 💚

  • Flexible, durable stems that hold shape beautifully
  • Custom colors to match any bouquet
  • Leaves & vines that add the perfect finishing touch
